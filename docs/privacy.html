Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · applies to Times Table Dojo 2.0 and later

The short version

  • We don't collect any personal data. None.
  • Everything the app stores stays on your device.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party code.
  • No accounts, and the app never connects to our servers (we don't have any).

What stays on your device

To work, the app saves a few things locally on the device. They are never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Player profiles: the name you type for each player (a first name or nickname is plenty), and a profile colour.
  • Progress: stars, belts, Daily Dojo streaks, Big Test scores and a history of answers (which facts were right or tricky), used for the grown-ups heat map and to pick practice questions.
  • Settings: the chosen language, music and sound effect switches, and VS Mode table choices.
  • Purchase status: whether the Full Dojo is unlocked, so the app works offline.
  • Rating reminder: a simple count of sessions and dates, so Apple's "rate this app" prompt isn't shown too often.

This data is included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups, like any app's data, under your control. It is not synced or shared. Deleting a profile deletes its progress; deleting the app deletes everything.

No ads, analytics or third parties

Times Table Dojo has no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, no social features and no third-party SDKs. It doesn't use device identifiers, location, contacts, the camera or the microphone. It works fully offline.

The only exception is Apple's own system: if you've chosen to "Share with App Developers" in your device's Analytics & Improvements settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. That's controlled by you in Settings, not by the app.

Purchases

The optional Full Dojo unlock is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is made for children and collects no personal information from them. Anything that involves money or leaves the app is behind a grown-up check (typing a number written in words as digits): buying or restoring the Full Dojo, opening the Grown-ups area, and sharing a belt certificate. Sharing uses the standard iOS share sheet, and only goes where a grown-up chooses to send it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. We'll never start collecting data without saying so here first.
