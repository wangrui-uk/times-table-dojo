How can we help?

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Send us a message.

🔑How do I unlock the Full Dojo?

Open Games and tap the Full Dojo card, tap any table with a gold key on it, or tap Unlock Full Dojo on a locked belt or the Big Test. A grown-up check appears first, then the usual Apple purchase sheet.

It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks all the tables from 1 to 12, every belt up to Black plus the Dans, the Big Test and best-of-3 VS battles.

🎁Which parts are free?

Everyone can play the 1, 2, 5 and 10 tables, earn belts up to Yellow, and try one free practice run of the Big Test.

🔄How do I restore my purchase?

On a new device or after reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases. You'll find it on the Full Dojo screen and in the Grown-ups area (Games → Grown-ups). Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

👨‍👩‍👧Does it work with Family Sharing?

Yes. When Family Sharing is turned on for purchases, everyone in your family group gets the Full Dojo. On a family member's device, open the app and tap Restore Purchases if it doesn't unlock straight away.

💛I bought Times Table Dojo before. Do I need to pay again?

No. If you bought the app when it was a paid download, everything is already unlocked for you, with nothing to buy. If it doesn't show as unlocked, tap Restore Purchases.

🛡️What is the grown-up check?

Before buying, restoring, opening the Grown-ups area or sharing a belt certificate, the app shows a number written in words and asks for it in digits. After three wrong tries it waits 30 seconds before letting anyone try again.

🧒How do I add or remove a player profile?

Tap the profile button at the top right of the home screen, then Create New Profile and type a name. To remove one, tap the bin next to it and confirm. Deleting a profile also deletes that player's progress.

🔇How do I turn the music or sound effects off?

Use the round buttons at the top left of the home screen: the music note turns the music on or off, and the speaker does the same for sound effects. The app also stays quiet when your device is on silent.

📱How does it work on iPhone Duo?
  • Closed: just like on any iPhone.
  • Opened flat: the app fills the big inner screen.
  • Half-open, sitting like a little laptop: arcade mode, with the question on the top half and big answer tiles on the bottom half.

Board Break and VS Mode need the phone flat, so in arcade mode the app asks you to lay it flat to carry on.

💾Where is my child's progress saved? Can it sync?

Progress is saved only on the device. There are no accounts and nothing is sent to us, so progress doesn't sync between devices. It's included in your device's normal iCloud or computer backup, so it comes back if you restore that backup. Deleting the app deletes its progress.

🛠️Something isn't working
  1. Make sure you have the latest version from the App Store.
  2. Fully close the app (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again.
  3. Restart your device.
  4. Purchase not showing? Check you're signed in with the right Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases.
  5. Still stuck? Email us: tell us your device, its iOS version and what happened.
